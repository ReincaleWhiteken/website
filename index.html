Unit 01 · Parts of Speech

Every Word Has a Job!

Just like aliens in a spaceship have different roles, every word in a sentence has a special job. Let's discover them with Blip, Zorp, Lila & Nano!

✅ Identify 8 parts of speech ✅ Use them in sentences ✅ Take the quiz!

🌍 Nouns – The Name of Things

👾
BLIP says:
A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, or idea! Like: alien, planet, rocket, happiness. Everything in our spaceship has a noun!
rocket
THING
The rocket flew fast.
Nano
PERSON
Nano is smart.
planet
PLACE
The planet is blue.
friendship
IDEA
Friendship is magic.

⚡ Verbs – The Action Words

🤖
ZORP says:
A verb shows an action or a state of being! Like: fly, eat, is, explore. Without verbs, nothing HAPPENS!
fly
ACTION
The aliens fly to Mars.
is
STATE
Lila is happy.
ate
PAST
Blip ate the jelly.
explore
ACTION
We explore space.

🎨 Adjectives & Adverbs – The Describers

🌸
LILA says:
Adjectives describe NOUNS — they answer "what kind?" or "how many?"
Adverbs describe VERBS — they answer "how?" "when?" or "where?"

Example: The colorful (adj) sand shone brightly (adv).
WordTypeDescribesExample
beautifulAdjectiveNoun (sand)The beautiful sand glowed.
colorfulAdjectiveNoun (planet)A colorful planet appeared.
quicklyAdverbVerb (ran)Zorp ran quickly.
yesterdayAdverbVerb (went)We went yesterday.
veryAdverbAdjective (soft)The towel was very soft.

Unit 02 · Sentence Structure

Build Your Sentences!

A sentence is like building a rocket — every part must be in the right place! Learn how to build simple, compound, and complex sentences with Zorp & Lila.

✅ Understand S-V-O pattern ✅ Build 3 types of sentences ✅ Identify sentence parts

🔩 The Basic Blueprint: Subject + Verb + Object

🤖
ZORP says:
Every English sentence needs at least a Subject and a Verb. The Subject is WHO does the action. The Verb is the action. The Object is WHO receives the action!
EXAMPLE SENTENCES:
Nano

+
the jelly

Lila

🌟 Simple Sentences

🌸
LILA says:
A simple sentence has ONE subject and ONE verb (one independent clause). Short, clear, and powerful! Like a laser beam — focused and direct!
Blip jumped.
1 subject + 1 verb
The rocket flew fast.
S + V + Adv
Zorp made a robot.
S + V + O
She is happy.
S + Linking V + Adj

🔗 Compound Sentences

👾
BLIP says:
A compound sentence joins TWO simple sentences using a conjunction (and, but, so, or, yet). Both parts are equally important — like two aliens working together! Use a comma before the conjunction.
ConjunctionMeaningExample
andAdditionNano flew the rocket, and Lila watched the stars.
butContrastBlip ate jelly, but Zorp didn't like it.
soResultIt was cold, so Lila wore a jacket.
orChoiceYou can fly, or you can walk.

🌌 Complex Sentences

⚡
NANO says:
A complex sentence has ONE main clause + ONE subordinate clause (a dependent idea). We use subordinating conjunctions: because, when, although, if, after, before, since, while, unless.
Because it was raining, we stayed inside.
BECAUSE (reason)
When Zorp arrived, the party started.
WHEN (time)
Although Blip was tired, he kept studying.
ALTHOUGH (contrast)
Lila practices if she has time.
IF (condition)

❓ Positive, Negative & Question Forms

TypePatternExample
✅ PositiveS + V + OI went to school.
❌ NegativeS + did not + V(base)I did not go to school.
❓ QuestionDid + S + V(base)?Did you go to school?
🌸
LILA reminds you:
In Simple Past tense, use did not (didn't) for negatives and Did...? for questions. The main verb goes back to its base form!

⚡ What are Irregular Verbs?

⚡
NANO says:
Regular verbs add -ed in the past: walk → walked, play → played.

But irregular verbs change their whole form! They don't follow rules — they're rebels! Like:

go → went   eat → ate   see → saw

👁️ Senses Scene — Irregular Verbs in Action

🌸
LILA – SIGHT 👁️
Yesterday I saw colorful sand. see → saw = to look at
👾
BLIP – TOUCH ✋
Yesterday I felt a soft towel. feel → felt = to touch / sense
⚡
NANO – SMELL 👃
I had a nice melon smell! have → had = to possess / experience
🤖
ZORP – CREATE 🏰
Yesterday I made a sand castle! make → made = to create / build
